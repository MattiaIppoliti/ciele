## Assistant setup

Open an Assistant to use these sections:

- **Preview** opens the live draft in a full page, the only preview available on a small screen.
- **General** configures identity, model, answer style, and chat defaults.
- **Knowledge** manages the Sources this Assistant answers from.
- **Flows** controls routing, triggers, conditions, and actions.
- **Tools & Skills** controls runtime tools and reusable instructions.
- **Goals** manages scheduled answer checks.
- **Guardrails** checks Visitor messages and hides marked answer text.
- **Assistant Help Desks** connects available Help Desks.
- **Style** controls the brand color and launcher position.
- **Authentication** controls the Visitor sign-in requirement.
- **Publish** manages the widget snapshot and embed configuration.

## Organization operations

Use the main navigation to open **Inbox**, **Improvements**, **Insights**, **Library**, and **Alerts**.

**Library** is the Organization view of Knowledge. It lists every Source and controls which Assistants answer from each one. See [Library](/knowledge/library).

## Find

Select the search icon or press **F** to open **Find**. Before you type, **Find** lists your 12 most recent items, then the console pages and the Assistant setup sections. Type to search Assistants, Conversations, Improvements, Help Desks, and Teammates. **Find** also lists the console pages and the Assistant setup sections.

Use the filters below the search box to narrow the results:

- **Title only** searches titles and ignores the text of each result.
- **Type** shows one kind of result.
- **Updated** shows results that changed today, in the past 7 days, or in the past 30 days.

The panel on the right draws the selected result as a small page. Key details appear inside it. For example, it shows the status of an Improvement, the channels of a Help Desk, or the first question of a Conversation. Ciele does not load the full page. Select a card or a row inside the preview to open that item. Press **Enter** to open it. Press **Cmd+Enter** or **Ctrl+Enter** to open it in a new tab.

## Settings

Use **Settings** to manage General Organization data, Members, AI Provider connections, Crawling, API Keys, Usage, and Billing.

Select **Personal settings** at the end of the **Settings** group to open your Profile, Theme, and Memory. The account menu also opens your Profile.

The available actions depend on your member Role.
