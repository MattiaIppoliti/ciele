---
title: Forms and availability
description: Collect required escalation data and show when a channel is available.
---

## Add form fields

Add only the data that the receiving team needs. Mark a field as required when the channel cannot operate without it.

Test the form as a Visitor. Confirm that required fields prevent an incomplete submission.

## Attach Conversation data

A Help Desk can attach configured Conversation information to an escalation. Available data can include a summary, history, user data, and metadata.

Select the minimum data required by the receiving team. Review the selection against your privacy requirements.

## Configure availability

Set the channel schedule and timezone. The widget shows whether the channel is available and can show its next available time.

Use the timezone of the team that receives the escalation. Test the boundary at the start and end of one availability window.

## Email delivery

The Email channel requires a configured Resend transport. When it is absent, Ciele reports that the email was not sent.
