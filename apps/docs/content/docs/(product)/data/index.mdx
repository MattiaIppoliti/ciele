## Two kinds of memory

**Session memory** belongs to one Conversation. It does not follow a Visitor into a new Conversation.

**Long-term memory** belongs to a verified SSO subject within one Organization. It can support later Conversations when the Organization enables it.

<Callout title="Signed-in Visitors only">
Anonymous Visitors never produce or recall long-term memories.
</Callout>
