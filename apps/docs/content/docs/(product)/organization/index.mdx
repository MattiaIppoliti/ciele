Your organization is the workspace that owns everything you build in Ciele: your
assistants, your help desks, your data, and your team. This section covers how to
manage the people in that workspace and your own account.

## Your organization

Everything you create belongs to one organization, and data stays isolated between
organizations. Organization level settings, such as who can access the workspace
and in what role, are managed by members with the right permissions.

## Your account

Your account is how you sign in and appear to your team. From your account you can
manage your profile and your sign in details. Keeping your profile accurate helps
teammates recognize your activity across the workspace, for example when you are
assigned an [improvement](/operations/improvements).
