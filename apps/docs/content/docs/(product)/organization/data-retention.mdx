---
title: Data retention
description: Set how long conversations, messages, improvements and audit records are kept, and hold one conversation back.
---

An Organization sets its retention periods in **Settings**, on the **Data retention** tab. Each data type has a **Keep for** period and an **Auto-delete** switch. **Auto-delete** is off by default for conversations, messages and improvements: nothing is deleted until an administrator turns it on.

## The data types

| Data type | What the nightly sweep does after the period |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversations** | Deletes the whole conversation and its messages. It also deletes the long-term memories about a Visitor that were not updated during the period. It also deletes the activity of Projects and Tasks that is older than the period. |
| **Messages** | Deletes old messages from conversations that stay. The transcript in the **Inbox** shows that earlier messages were deleted. |
| **Improvements** | Deletes improvements that were not updated during the period, whatever their status. |
| **Audit records** | Deletes entries of the audit log that are older than the period. This type is **Always on**. The default period is 180 days. |

Each period is between 1 month and 5 years. One month is 30 days and one year is 365 days. A period outside these limits shows an error, and **Save changes** stays unavailable.

The data types are independent. For example, keep conversations for two years but delete messages after six months.

## How the periods are measured

- A conversation's period starts at its last message, not at its first message. With a 30-day period, a conversation that started 3 months ago and received its last message 10 days ago is kept for 20 more days.
- A message's period starts when the message is sent.
- An improvement's period starts at its last update.

## What deletion does

- The sweep runs once per night.
- Deletion is permanent. The deleted data does not go to a trash area and you cannot recover it.
- Deleted data no longer shows in the **Inbox**, in **Insights**, in exports, in chat history or in **Improvements**. The audit log never records the content of messages or conversations.
- When the sweep deletes a message, the linked improvements stay. They no longer show that message.
- When you turn **Auto-delete** off, the sweep stops. Data that the sweep deleted before does not come back.

<Callout type="warn" title="Retention deletes data">
Export what you must keep before you turn on **Auto-delete** or shorten a period.
</Callout>

## Set a period

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select the **Data retention** tab.
3. Turn on **Auto-delete** for the data type.
4. Type a number under **Keep for** and select **Days**, **Months** or **Years**.
5. Select **Save changes**.

If the change starts a deletion or makes a period shorter, Ciele asks you to confirm. The next nightly sweep deletes the older data. Select **Cancel** to go back to the saved settings.

Only a member who can manage members can change these settings. Each change shows in the audit log.

## Reasoning traces

The **General** tab has one more window, **Reasoning trace retention**. After that window, the nightly sweep removes the Thinking panel from old messages. The messages stay.

## Legal hold

A legal hold exempts one conversation from the retention sweep. The sweep does not delete the conversation or its messages. Use a hold when a preservation obligation applies to one conversation, without turning retention off for every other conversation.

1. Open **Inbox** and select the conversation.
2. Open the details panel.
3. Select **Place legal hold** in the **Retention** card.

The hold stays until a member selects **Release legal hold**. Every member sees the hold status. Only a member who can manage members can place or release a hold.

A held conversation cannot be deleted. This applies to the Inbox, the API, the retention sweep, and the deletion of the assistant that owns the conversation. To delete a held conversation or its assistant, release the hold first.

## Self-hosted installations

The nightly sweep runs from the bundled scheduler. No extra configuration is needed. The sweep calls `/api/cron/sweep-retention` with the `CRON_SECRET` credential.
