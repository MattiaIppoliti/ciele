---
title: General settings
description: Set the assistant's identity, welcome message, disclaimer, answering style, and conversation options.
---

The General section defines who the assistant is and how it presents itself. Open
an assistant, then select **General** in the setup rail.

## Identity

- **Assistant title** is the name of the assistant.
- **Nickname** is the short name shown in the widget header, where visitors see it.
- **Description** explains the assistant's purpose. Keep it clear and concise.

## Welcome message

The welcome message is the first thing a visitor reads when they open the chat.
You write it in a rich text editor, so you can use bold, italics, headings,
lists, quotes, and links. Use it to set expectations: what the assistant can help
with, and how to get to a human if needed.

## AI disclaimer

The disclaimer appears under the assistant's answers. Use it to remind visitors
that responses are generated by AI and to point them to authoritative sources
when accuracy matters.

## Answering style

The answering style is a free text guide to tone and format. Describe how you want
the assistant to sound and structure its replies, for example short and friendly,
or formal and thorough. This guidance is applied on top of the platform's own
instructions, so it shapes every generated answer without overriding safety rules.

<Callout title="Verbatim vs generated">
Fixed messages you set in a Flow are shown word for word. The answering style
only affects answers the assistant generates, such as those produced when it
searches your knowledge. See [Flow actions](/flows/actions).
</Callout>

## Starter buttons

Starter buttons are quick replies shown at the start of a conversation. They give
visitors an easy way in. Each button has a type that decides what it does. See
[Starter buttons](/assistants/quick-replies) for the full setup.

## Conversation options

The Configuration group holds toggles that change the chat experience. Depending
on your setup these can include the chat window language, a progress view that
rewrites the assistant's thinking into short, readable updates, and other
behaviors. Turn on only what your visitors will benefit from, and confirm each
one in the live preview.

## The chat launcher

The **Enable chat launcher** toggle controls whether the floating chat button
appears. Turn it off if you plan to open the assistant in another way, such as
from your own button, and turn it on for the standard floating widget.
