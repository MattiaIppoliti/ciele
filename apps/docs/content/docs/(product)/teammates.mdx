An **AI Teammate** is an AI colleague that your members chat with inside Ciele. It uses the same
answer engine as your Assistant by default. An admin can connect an external agent harness.
It answers your team, not your Visitors.

A Teammate has no Publication, no widget, and no Help Desk. Nothing about it reaches your website.
With a configured channel connection, members can also chat with it in Slack, Telegram or Microsoft Teams.

## Chat

Select **Chat** at the top of the sidebar, or open **Teammates**. The sidebar then shows your
Teammates, your groups and your conversations. The chat opens in the middle of the page.

- **AI Teammates.** The face of each Teammate. Select a face to open its chat. Right-click a face to
  configure it, copy its ID, hide it or delete it.
- **Groups.** Your channels. Select **+** to open a new group.
- **Conversations.** Your conversations with every Teammate, newest first, one folder for each day.

Select **New chat** at the bottom of the sidebar to start a new conversation with Ciele AI. The
button is there on every page. You can also press `⌘O` on a Mac, or `Ctrl+O` on Windows and Linux.

## Ciele AI

Every organization has one **Ciele AI**. It is the AI layer of the whole console, and Chat opens on
it. Ciele creates it the first time a member opens Chat.

Ciele AI acts for the member who chats with it, with the permissions of that member. It cannot do
more than that member can do in the console. For example, a Viewer cannot create an Assistant with it.

- **Knowledge.** It searches every Knowledge Collection that you can read.
- **Actions.** It can use the operations that the CLI, the MCP server and the API use. It cannot
  approve a stopped action, it cannot delete an API key, and it cannot set a credential. If a request
  contains a password, a token or a key, Ciele AI stops the request. Enter the credential in the
  console.
- **Suggested Fixes.** When Ciele AI accepts a Suggested Fix, the FAQ gets the machine-confirmed
  trust level, not human-reviewed.
- **Confirmation.** Some actions wait for you. Ciele AI shows a card, and the action runs only when
  you approve it. These actions wait:
  - An action that deletes, removes, revokes or publishes.
  - An action that changes access: a role change, an invitation, the SSO identity claim, or the
    permissions of a Teammate.
  - An Eval run.
  - A change to how long the organization keeps transcripts or traces.

Ciele AI has no Standing role. You can rename it and write a Standing role in **Configure**, the
same as for a Teammate. You cannot delete it or hide it, and it is always visible to all members.

To ask a Teammate from Ciele AI, write **@** and select the Teammate. A tag with its name shows
above the message box. Write your question and send it. The question goes to that Teammate, and
its chat opens.

## Create a Teammate

Open **Chat** and select **New** under **AI Teammates**. You need the Editor role or higher.
The creation form opens in the central panel. It uses the same section timeline as **Configure**.
Select **Create teammate** in the top bar or at the end of the form.

Each Teammate has three parts.

- **Name and title.** The name starts every chat. The title says what the Teammate does.
- **Standing role.** Write what the Teammate is for, in your own words. Brief it like a colleague.
- **Knowledge.** Select the Knowledge Collections and the single Library items that the Teammate
  can search.

The Standing role applies to the next message. A Teammate has no Publication, so you never publish
a change.

### The avatar

Ciele draws the avatar. Each Teammate gets a figure generated from its identity. It looks the same
on the roster, in the chat header and in a transcript, and you upload nothing.

The drawing is deterministic, not random: the same Teammate always gets the same face. Select
**Change colour** in **Configure** to get a different one, and the face then survives a rename.

Members work the same way. Anybody who has not uploaded a photo gets a generated figure. It shows
in the member list, in the sidebar and beside an Improvement they are assigned. A row is then
recognisable at a glance, and a real photo always wins over the generated figure.

## Knowledge scope

The Knowledge scope below applies to the Ciele harness. An external harness supplies its own knowledge and tools.

A Teammate searches only what you select. You select in two ways, and you can use both together.

- **Collections.** A whole Knowledge Collection. Use this for a subject that grows over time.
- **Single items.** One website, one file, or one FAQ from your Library. Use this to give a
  Teammate two documents, and not the whole collection around them.

The picker has one tab for **Collections**, and one tab for each Library type: **Websites**,
**Files**, **Applications** and **FAQs**. The number on a tab tells you how many items you selected
there.

Ciele searches your Collections and your single items together, and it shows the best answers from
both. Answers cite a Document and its Source, exactly like the widget.

An empty selection is a valid configuration. The Teammate then answers from its Standing role only.
It has no search tool at all, and it says so when a question needs a Source.

<Callout title="You do not need to select an item twice">
An item inside a Collection that you selected is already in the scope. The picker says which
Collection covers it, so you can leave it clear.
</Callout>

If you delete a collection or an item that a Teammate still searches, Ciele creates an Alert of the
**Knowledge** type. The Teammate keeps answering from its role, and it finds nothing there. Open
**Configure** and remove the dead entry from its knowledge to clear the Alert.

<Callout title="Same Knowledge, no copy">
A Teammate reads the Library. It does not hold a separate copy. New content in a selected
Collection is available in the next message.
</Callout>

## Execution

An Organization admin controls execution in **Configure → Execution**. New Teammates use the Ciele harness, with internet and computer capabilities off.

- **Agent harness.** Choose Ciele or an external AG-UI harness registered by your deployment operator.
- **Public internet.** Read public pages to research current information.
- **Browser.** Browse with the Teammate's persistent browser profile. This requires internet access and a configured computer service.
- **Workspace files.** Keep files in the Teammate's own workspace across computer restarts.
- **Terminal.** Run commands inside its computer. This requires internet, workspace files and an edit capability ceiling.

Computer actions run autonomously with these permissions. Ciele operations keep their existing action approvals.
All members who can use a Teammate share its workspace and browser profile.

Select **Check computer**, **Start** or **Stop** to control its computer after you save permissions.
Cancelling an active computer operation stops the computer. Its files remain available after a restart.
Your deployment operator must configure the computer service before these controls become available.

An external AG-UI harness receives the Standing role, permitted memory and recent text conversation.
It controls its own tools, permissions and billing. Ciele records its answer and tool receipts in the transcript.
Ciele's Knowledge scope, model selection, computer permissions and action grants do not configure that external harness.
External harnesses currently support text conversations. Use the Ciele harness for attachments and native action approvals.

## Other clients and messaging channels

An AG-UI client can connect through `POST /api/v1/teammates/{id}/ag-ui`, using a Ciele API key.
Ciele restores the Member's persisted history and checks their current permissions.

Your deployment operator can connect Slack, Telegram and Microsoft Teams through Chat SDK adapters.
Each connection specifies the Teammate, permitted channels and platform users mapped to existing Ciele members.
An unmapped user cannot start a Teammate conversation. A private Teammate answers only in a direct message.
Answers posted in a group are visible to that group's participants.

Channel conversations use the Ciele harness or the selected external harness. Ciele keeps a separate history for each Member.
Open Ciele to review the transcript and decide pending action approvals. Channel attachments and native approval buttons are not bridged.

## Who can see a Teammate

Visibility has two values.

- **The whole organization.** Every member sees the Teammate on the roster.
- **Only me and admins.** The owner, the members that the owner names as editors, and the
  Organization admins see it.

The owner, the named editors, and the admins can change the configuration. A member with the Viewer
role can chat with a Teammate, but cannot create or change one.

### Hide one from your own roster

A long roster is somebody else's problem to solve, so each member can shorten their own.
Right-click a Teammate in the sidebar and select **Hide from my roster**.

This changes nothing for anybody else. The Teammate keeps answering your colleagues, it can still be
suggested to you as a referral, and you can still open it from a link. Only your roster gets
shorter. Open **Hidden from your roster** at the bottom of the sidebar to put one back.

<Callout title="Hiding is not deleting">
Deleting a Teammate retires it for the whole organization. Hiding shortens one list, and only yours.
</Callout>

## What a Teammate can do

A new Teammate can only talk. It answers questions and it changes nothing.

To let it act, an **admin** grants it one or more domains. A grant is a row. No row means no
permission, and the Teammate does not even see the tool.

| Domain | What the grant permits |
| --- | --- |
| **Improvements** | Read the board, move items, and draft Suggested Fixes for flagged answers. |
| **Knowledge** | Read the Library, and add or correct FAQs. It never deletes a Source. |
| **Inbox** | Read Visitor conversations and pin them. It never deletes one. |
| **Flows** | Read an Assistant's Flows and hand back drafts and proposals to the Flow Canvas. It never saves a Flow. The console grants this to the Flows Agent it creates. See [Flow Canvas](/flows/canvas). |

Open **Configure** to set the grants. Only an admin sees the controls. Every other member sees one
sentence that says what the Teammate can do. The permissions of an agent are not a secret from the
people who work with it.

### How far it can go

Each Teammate has one limit that applies to all of its grants.

- **Read only.** It can look at everything you granted. It changes none of it.
- **Read and change.** The default. It can change things inside the domains you granted.

A Teammate never publishes or manages members, API keys, or roles. Those permissions do not exist
for a Teammate.

### Accept a Suggested Fix

A Teammate with the **Improvements** grant can draft a Suggested Fix. A person must accept it. The fix
becomes knowledge only after that.

An admin can change this for one Teammate with the **Let it accept its own Suggested Fixes**
control. The control needs the **Improvements** grant and the **Knowledge** grant. It is off by default.

With the control on, a fix that the Teammate drafts becomes a FAQ in your Knowledge, and nobody
reviews it first. Ciele records the Teammate as the author. The Document then shows the
machine-confirmed trust level, not human-reviewed.

<Callout type="warn" title="Read this before you turn it on">
A Teammate that accepts its own fixes writes to your Knowledge with no human review.
</Callout>

### The transcript is the record

Every action shows in the conversation as a card. The card names the operation and the item that
it changed. Ciele records the member who asked, not the Teammate, on the change itself.

## What a Teammate remembers

A Teammate reads three documents at the start of every message. Each one is
short, and you can read and change all three.

| Document | Who it is about | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your profile** | You. One document, shared with every Teammate you talk to. | You, and the Teammates you talk to. |
| **What it has learned** | The Teammate's own notes about doing its job. | The owner and the editors of the Teammate. |
| **Decisions** | What your team has settled. | Members with the Editor role or higher. |

Each document has a limit of 4000 characters. A Teammate reads the whole
document, so an empty one costs nothing and adds nothing.

### Your profile

Open **Settings**, then **Memory**. This document says how you work: your
timezone, the words you use, what you want short and what you want in full.

A Teammate adds to it when it learns something about you. Every change shows in
the **History** list with the name of the Teammate that made it and the date.
Select **Undo this change** to put the document back the way it was. Nobody
else can read this document, not even an administrator.

When you leave the Organization, or an administrator removes you, Ciele deletes
your profile and its history. If you join again, you start with an empty
profile.

### What it has learned

At the end of a conversation, a Teammate decides if it learned something
durable about its job. Most conversations teach nothing, and the Teammate then
writes nothing. When it does write, it adds one line, with a date.

A durable fact is a convention your organization uses, a name you have for
something, or a correction a colleague made. It is not what you discussed
today.

Open **Configure** on the Teammate to read these notes. Delete a line that is
wrong. The Teammate reads this document every message, so a wrong line stays
wrong until you correct it.

### Projects

A **Project** is a name, a description, and one document of decisions. Open
**Projects** to create one. You need the Editor role or higher.

Attach a Teammate to a project in **Configure**. A Teammate reads at most one
project. From the next message, it answers "what did we decide" from the
project document, and it can add a decision itself. Every write shows as a card
in the conversation.

Select **Archive** to stop a project. The decisions stay readable, and attached
Teammates stop reading them. Select the delete icon to remove the project and
its decisions. Archive is the safer choice, because it keeps the record.

## Referral to another Teammate

A Teammate answers from its own role and its own knowledge. When your question
belongs to a colleague, it says so and shows a card with:

- the name of the other Teammate,
- one sentence on why that one,
- a summary of your conversation, so you do not repeat yourself.

Select **Continue with …** on the card. Ciele opens a chat with that Teammate,
and gives it the summary. The two conversations keep a link to each other.

The other Teammate answers from **its** knowledge and **its** grants. It does
not get the knowledge or the permissions of the Teammate that referred you.

<Callout title="A referral is an offer, not a handover">
The other Teammate does not speak in the first conversation, and nothing
happens until you select the card. You stay in control of the handoff.
</Callout>

A Teammate refers only to Teammates that the whole organization can see. It
never names a private Teammate, and it shows no card at all when there is no
other Teammate to suggest.

## Channels

A **channel** is one thread for several members and several Teammates. Use a channel when a piece
of work needs more than one specialist.

Open **Chat** and select **+** next to **Groups**. Give the channel a name, then add the Teammates and
the people you want. Every member can open a channel. You do not need the Editor role.

You can also start a channel from a 1:1 chat. Write **@** in the message box and select a Teammate.
Ciele opens a new channel with both Teammates and with you. The 1:1 conversation does not move. It
stays where it is and you can read it again. Ciele AI does not join channels.

A channel is invite-based. Only the people in the channel can see it. A colleague who is not in the
channel cannot open it, and does not see it in the roster.

The header of a channel shows the face of each member. Select the faces, or select **Add**, to open
the list of the Teammates and the people that you can add. Select a name to add that Teammate, or to
invite that person.

### Ask a Teammate in a channel

Write `@` and the name of a Teammate. That Teammate answers in the thread.

A Teammate answers only when somebody names it. A Teammate that nobody names stays silent. There is
no default answerer.

A Teammate can name another Teammate in its answer. That Teammate then answers on its own, in the
same thread. One question can reach the whole team, and you read one thread.

A Teammate can name only the Teammates in the channel. It cannot reach a Teammate outside the
channel, and it cannot reach an Assistant.

### Where a chain stops

A **chain** is everything that one message starts. A chain has two limits:

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Teammate answers from one message | 10 |
| Answers from one Teammate | 2 |

The chain stops at a limit. The thread then shows a line that explains why. Send another message to
continue.

<Callout title="Why there is a limit">
Two Teammates can answer each other. The limits make sure that they stop, and that one question
costs you one conversation.
</Callout>

### Each Teammate keeps its own permissions

Each Teammate answers with its own role, its own knowledge scope, and its own grants. A channel
gives no Teammate more permission.

Ciele records the member who sent the first message as the person who asked. Every change that a
Teammate makes in the chain is recorded against that member.

A Teammate reads the profile of that member only. It does not read the profile of the other people
in the channel.

### Give a channel a Project

A channel can use one **Project**. Select the settings icon in the channel, then choose the
Project. Every Teammate in the channel then reads the decisions document of that Project, and
writes the decisions of the channel back to it.

### New messages

The roster shows the channels with new messages. Ciele marks a channel with **Mentioned you** only
when a message names you. Messages between Teammates do not mark it.

### When somebody names you

If a message names you, Ciele tells you outside the channel too. You get a card at the bottom right
of the screen, and a count on the **Teammates** row in the sidebar.

**You get one card for each channel, and no more.** Three colleagues can name you ten times in the
same channel. That is one place to go and look, so it is one card. The count in the sidebar is the
number of channels that wait for you, not the number of times somebody named you.

Open the channel to clear the card and the count. There is nothing else to dismiss.

<Callout title="A Teammate does not get a card">
A Teammate that you name answers in the channel. The answer is the signal. Cards are for people.
</Callout>

### Read a channel as an Owner or Admin

An Owner or an Admin can read every channel in the organization, also a channel that they are not
in. Open **Inbox**, then **Channels**.

Channels are internal. They stay out of the Visitor conversation list, and out of Insights.

## Routines

A **routine** is a standing instruction that a Teammate carries out on its own.
Open **Configure** and select **Add** under **Routines**. A Teammate can have 5.

Each routine has an instruction and a schedule. The schedule is a preset:

| Cadence | When it runs |
| --- | --- |
| **Every day** | Each day, at the hour you choose. |
| **Every Monday** | Each Monday, at the hour you choose. |
| **The 1st of each month** | The first day of the month, at the hour you choose. |

The hour is UTC. Ciele runs each routine one time per period. If the schedule
is late, the routine still runs one time, and it does not run twice to catch up.

<Callout title="The hour depends on your scheduler">
A self-hosted Ciele checks for due routines every hour, and runs each one at the
hour you chose. On Vercel, the Hobby plan permits one check each day. All
routines then run at that daily check, and the hour you chose is approximate.
The Pro plan removes the limit.
</Callout>

<Callout title="A routine acts on its grants, and on nothing more">
An unattended run has no member behind it. The Teammate uses only the domains
you granted it, at the ceiling you set. A routine on a Teammate with no grants
can read and answer, and change nothing.
</Callout>

### Each run is a conversation

Every run adds a conversation to the thread of the person who created the
routine. The card at the top of the list says **Routine**, so you can see which
work was unattended. Each action shows as a card, the same as in a chat.

### When a run fails

A failed run creates an **Alert**. The alert says which routine failed and why.
The next successful run clears it. A quiet failure is the risk with unattended
work, so Ciele makes the failure loud.

If you delete the Teammate, its routines stop. This is not a failure, and it
creates no alert.

### Triage visitor feedback

The most useful routine scans the answers your visitors rated down and files
the new problems on the **Improvements** board.

Give the Teammate the **Improvements** grant, then add a routine like this:

> Every morning, triage yesterday's thumbs-down and file what is new.

Each run:

- reads the conversations with a thumbs-down from the last day,
- attaches the answer to an improvement that is already open for that
  conversation, instead of creating a second one,
- creates a maximum of 5 new improvements,
- adds the `auto-filed` tag to each new item, so you can filter the board.

The limit of 5 applies to new items only. A problem you already know about
always gets the new evidence.

## Organize chat rows

Swipe a history or group row to the left, or select its **Actions** button.
A full swipe archives the row. The action drawer also offers **Flag** and **Delete**.
Use **Archived** to restore a row, or **Flagged** to find marked rows.
These preferences belong to you and remain saved after a reload.
Archiving a group leaves its shared transcript and the other members' lists intact.
Deleting a group requires confirmation and removes it for everyone. Only its creator or an Organization admin can delete it.
You can delete only your own Teammate conversations. Row actions wait while that chat streams a response.

## Conversations

Each member has a private thread with each Teammate. Select a conversation under **Conversations**
in the sidebar to open it again. You can also select **View history** in the chat header.

For a long answer, open the **More options** menu in the chat header and select **Open full
screen**. The chat then fills the window. Select **Exit full screen**, or press `Escape`, to go
back.

Teammate conversations stay out of the Inbox, because the Inbox is the queue for Visitor
conversations. They also stay out of Insights, because internal use must not change your widget
metrics. Channels are in the Inbox, in their own list, for an Owner or an Admin.

## Automation

The `teammates` domain is available to the CLI, the MCP server, and the API. An API key acts as
the Member who created it, so it sees the same Teammates that member sees.

```bash
ciele teammates list
ciele teammates create --name "Nora" --title "Support Copywriter"
```

Channels have a `channels` domain of their own. You can read a channel, open one,
and change who is in it:

```bash
ciele channels list
ciele channels create --name "Launch week" --teammates <teammate-id>
ciele channels add-member <channel-id> <user-id>
```

There is no command to send a message into a channel. A message starts a chain of
teammate replies, and a chain runs in the console, where you can watch it.

## Which AI provider answers

A Teammate uses the Organization's provider connections, like your Assistant.

If your Organization allows personal AI subscriptions and you paired your own device, your own
chats with a Teammate can use your subscription. This applies to your chats only. A colleague who
chats with the same Teammate uses the Organization's connections.

A channel always uses the Organization's connections. A channel has several people in it, so it is
not personal use.

### Attach a file

Select **+** in the message box, then **Attach a file**. You can also drag a file onto the message
box, or paste an image into it. A Teammate chat accepts attachments at all times, because you are
signed in.

Ciele reads the file into text and answers from that text. Ciele does not keep the file. An image
goes to your Organization's AI provider, which reads it. This costs one AI call.

The attachment stays available for the rest of the conversation. Remove it from the strip above the
message box when you no longer need it.

### Set the default model

Open **Configure**, then **Models**, and select the provider and **Default model**.
Save the settings. The default applies to the next message and does not change the additional
models that colleagues may choose. You can also set the default for Ciele AI in your Organization.

### Select a model for one message

Select **Configure**, then select the models in **Models it can answer with**. The Teammate model is
always available. Each additional model that you select becomes an option in the chat window.

If you select no additional model, the chat window shows no model control.

**Auto** is the first option and the default. Auto uses the most accurate model of your latest
[Eval](/operations/eval) on the answer stage, if a connection of your organization can use that model.
Before an Eval ranks your models, Auto uses the Teammate model.

Ciele AI always shows the model control. It lists every model, and it marks the models that no
connection serves as **not connected**.

Your own AI subscription has priority. If you paired your own device, your chats use your
subscription. The chat window model does not apply. Change your subscription in **Settings**, then
**AI**.

## Interactive table replies

Teammates can show CRM records grids and task tables with status filters when their data supports these views.
Select rows, sort or resize CRM columns, and use task status chips to filter displayed rows.

For a new CRM property, open **New property**, review the prompt, and select **Go calculate**.
The request enters your Teammate conversation and follows its existing permissions. The UI never invents property results.
The table remains part of the saved conversation. Display controls do not change records in connected systems.

## View the workspace

Computer actions add an **Agent screen** card inside the Teammate's reply while it works.
The card shows current browser captures and the operation's status. Select **Open** to expand the screen.
Terminal actions show their command and recorded result below the card.
Closing the screen keeps the Teammate working. Earlier replies retain their action receipts and link to the current screen.
They do not contain a screen recording of the earlier run.

Select **Workspace** in a Teammate chat to open Browser, Terminal and Files.
On desktop, the viewer opens in the resizable right sidebar beside the chat.
It shares the **Developer** panel's space. Opening either panel closes the other.
On phones and touch tablets, the viewer opens full-screen. Use the close control to hide the viewer.

**Browser** shows fresh captures of the Teammate's browser, its current URL and the capture time.
The screen updates while visible. Opening the viewer does not start a stopped computer. If the
computer service is not connected, Ciele shows that state instead of a sample screen.

**Terminal** shows computer commands and their recorded results from the current conversation.
Use **Ask teammate** to request a command or a browser URL. The request runs through the usual chat,
model, tools and permission checks, and its result stays in the transcript.

**Files** lists the running computer's saved workspace files. Select **Refresh files**, then a file
to read it. File access must be enabled. Large files and lists show when the displayed content is
truncated.

Organization admins can **Start computer** or **Stop computer**. Stop interrupts computer work but
keeps its files and browser profile. A stopped computer must be started before its files or screen
can be viewed. Grant access in **Configure → Execution** and save the settings first.

## Delete a Teammate

Right-click the Teammate in the sidebar and select **Delete**. You can also select **Configure**,
then **Delete teammate**. The Teammate leaves every roster and nobody can chat with it again. You
cannot delete Ciele AI.

Your past conversations with it stay readable. The record of the work remains.

## React to a reply

Right-click an AI reply to open the emoji menu, or use **Add reaction** on touch
and keyboard. Choose a quick emoji, or open **More emoji** for the full picker.
The compact menu opens a full picker with categories and a search field.
Search accepts English names, common Italian names, and small typing mistakes.
You can add one reaction per reply. Choosing another emoji replaces your previous
reaction. Click your emoji again to remove it.

Reactions appear as badges on the reply. Hover or focus a badge to see who added
it. A count appears when several members choose the same emoji. Groups share
these reactions with their participants. The same controls are available in
Assistant Preview and the published widget. Anonymous widget visitors appear as
“Visitor”. Inbox transcripts show each emoji and its author without assigning a
sentiment or quality score. Social reactions do not change answer-quality feedback or analytics.
