Ciele is a platform for building AI assistants that live on your website and in
your apps. Each assistant answers questions using your own content, follows the
rules you define, and hands the conversation to your team when a human is the
right next step.

You configure everything from one admin console: what the assistant knows, how
it replies, when it escalates, and where it appears. A live preview shows the
assistant exactly as your visitors will see it while you make changes.

Ciele comes in two editions: an **open-source core** you can self-host under the
AGPL-3.0 license, and **Ciele Cloud**, the same product fully managed for you. The
guides in this section apply to both. See [Editions](/editions) for what each one
includes.

## What you can do with Ciele

- Create one assistant or many, each with its own identity, knowledge, and rules.
- Feed each assistant knowledge from your websites, your files, and curated
  question and answer pairs.
- Control conversations with Flows, an ordered set of rules that decides how the
  assistant responds to each message.
- Escalate to your team through Help Desks, with channels such as email, phone,
  live chat, and support tickets.
- Publish the assistant to your website with a small snippet, then review every
  conversation, track answer quality, and read analytics.

## Who this documentation is for

This documentation serves two audiences at once. If you are evaluating Ciele, the
early sections explain what the platform does and how the pieces fit together. If
you are already using Ciele, every section also shows you how to use the feature
inside the app, step by step. It applies whether you run the open-source edition
yourself or use Ciele Cloud.
