---
title: My Tasks
description: See the Tasks that are assigned to you in every Project.
---

**My Tasks** shows the Tasks that are assigned to you in all Projects that you can read. It includes subtasks.

The line below the title shows how many Tasks are open, due today, and overdue. It also shows how many Tasks you completed since Monday.

## Groups

My Tasks puts your Tasks in fixed groups:

| Group | Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| Overdue | The due date is in the past. |
| Today | The due date is today. |
| Upcoming | The due date is in the future. |
| No due date | There is no due date. |
| Completed | The status is **Done**. |

When you add a Task in a group, Ciele assigns it to you and sets the date of the group.

## Starred Tasks

To star a Task, open it and select the star. Select **Starred** in the toolbar to show only the Tasks that you starred. Select **Starred** again to show all your Tasks.

## Calendar

Select **Calendar** in the toolbar to see your Tasks on their due dates.

## Improvements

Improvements that are assigned to you also show at the top of My Tasks. Select an Improvement to open it.

## The sidebar badge

The badge on **My Tasks** shows how many of your open Tasks are due today or overdue.
