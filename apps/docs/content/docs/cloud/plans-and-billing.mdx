On Ciele Cloud each organization is on a subscription plan. The plan sets what
your organization can do and how much it can use in a month, and it is where
payment and invoices are handled.

## Choosing a plan

Plans differ mainly in their [usage allowances](/cloud/usage-and-limits) and in
the size of the team and number of assistants they suit. You can start on an entry
plan and move up as your usage grows; a change takes effect from the next billing
period.

## Managing payment

Billing is managed from the organization settings in the console:

- **Payment method.** Add or update the card on file for your organization.
- **Invoices.** Review and download past invoices for your records.
- **Plan changes.** Upgrade or change your plan; the console shows what changes
  and when it takes effect.

## Who can manage billing

Billing is an organization-level responsibility, so it is limited to members with
an administrative role. See [Members and roles](/organization/members-and-roles)
for how roles control access. A member without the right role can use the product
normally but will not see billing controls.

## When a plan reaches its limit

Plans include monthly usage allowances. As you approach and reach them, Ciele
meters usage and applies the plan's cap. What happens at the cap, and how to watch
your usage during the month, is covered in [Usage and limits](/cloud/usage-and-limits).
