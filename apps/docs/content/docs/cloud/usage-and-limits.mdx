<Callout title="Ciele Cloud only">
Usage metering and enforced limits are part of the managed edition. A self-hosted
open-source deployment has no usage caps — its capacity is bounded only by the
infrastructure and AI provider keys you configure.
</Callout>

Every Ciele Cloud plan includes a monthly usage allowance. Ciele meters your
organization's usage as conversations happen and compares it against your plan's
allowance, so there are no surprises and no manual counting.

## What counts as usage

Usage is measured by the AI work your assistants do on behalf of your visitors —
principally the answers assistants generate. The console shows your current usage
for the billing period so you always know where you stand.

## Watching your usage

You can see usage for the current period in the console at any time. Watch it if
you are running a campaign, launching a new assistant, or otherwise expecting a
spike, so you can move to a larger [plan](/cloud/plans-and-billing) before you run
out of allowance.

## Reaching the cap

As your usage nears the plan allowance, Ciele surfaces it in the console. When the
allowance is reached, the plan's cap applies for the rest of the billing period.
Upgrading to a plan with a larger allowance restores capacity immediately; the
allowance otherwise resets at the start of the next billing period.

## Keeping usage efficient

The same practices that make answers better also keep usage efficient: focused
[knowledge](/knowledge) so retrieval stays on target, [flows](/flows) that handle
common questions directly, and [help desks](/help-desks) that hand off cleanly
when a human is the right next step.
