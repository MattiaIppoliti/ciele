## What we operate for you

On Ciele Cloud, the running of the platform is handled by the Ciele team:

- **Hosting** of the console, the chat widget, and the AI runtime.
- **Upgrades** to new versions, applied for you with no action on your side.
- **Backups** of your organization's data.
- **Availability** monitoring so problems are noticed and worked without you
  having to report them.

This is the practical difference from self-hosting: the [Alerts](/operations/alerts)
you see in the console are about *your* integrations and content, not about the
health of the platform itself.

## Getting help

Reach the team through the support options in the console. When you get in touch,
it helps to include:

- The assistant involved, by name or identifier.
- A link to the relevant conversation in the [Inbox](/operations/inbox), if the
  question is about a specific answer.
- What you expected to happen and what happened instead.

## Where to look first

Many questions are answered faster from the console itself: the
[Inbox](/operations/inbox) shows exactly what a visitor asked and how the
assistant replied, [Improvements](/operations/improvements) tracks answer-quality
work, and [Insights](/operations/insights) shows the trends behind a report.
