# Contributor License Agreement

Ciele is open source under the AGPL-3.0-only license, and Ciele also offers a
commercial managed edition built from the same core. To be able to ship your
contribution in both, we need your permission — so every pull request to this
repository must be covered by this agreement.

Signing takes one comment: when you open your first pull request, a bot posts a
link, you click through, and the status check turns green. You only ever do this
once.

## The agreement

> I give Ciele permission to license my contributions on any terms they like. I
> am giving Ciele this license in order to make it possible for them to accept
> my contributions into their project.

That is the whole grant. In plain terms:

- **You keep your copyright.** This is a license, not an assignment — you can
  use and relicense your own work however you like, forever.
- **Ciele may ship your contribution under the AGPL** (as it does today) **and
  inside the commercial managed edition.** Without that permission, an accepted
  contribution would leave Ciele unable to distribute its own product.
- **You confirm you have the right to grant it** — the work is yours, or your
  employer permits you to contribute it.

## If your employer needs a formal document

Some employers require a signed corporate agreement rather than a click-through.
Open an issue titled "Corporate CLA" and we will send an Apache-ICLA-derived
form covering you or your organization; a signed copy replaces the click-through
for all your pull requests.

## Why a CLA at all

The core of Ciele is AGPL-3.0. If Ciele merged contributions it did not have
permission to relicense, Ciele would itself become a licensee of that code, and
the AGPL's network-use clause would apply to the managed service it operates
from this codebase. The CLA is what keeps the open-source edition and the
managed edition buildable from one shared core.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how contributions are reviewed and
credited.
